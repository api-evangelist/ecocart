# EcoCart

> EcoCart is a sustainable-commerce platform that bundles climate contributions, shipping protection, and returns management into the e-commerce checkout. It helps merchants reduce and communicate the carbon footprint of orders, offer package protection, and drive loyalty — with a developer API for programmatic integration. Backed by 500 Global.
